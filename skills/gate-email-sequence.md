# Skill: Gate Email Sequence

## Purpose
Write a 6-email sequence for a Circle of Silence gate purchase. Each gate has a wound profile (Guilty Giver, Depleted Survivor, Striving Achiever, Lost Wanderer). Emails speak directly to that wound.

## Rules
- KJV only — no NIV, no NLT, no paraphrase
- Wound profile specific — write to the specific woman, her specific pain
- 150-250 words per email
- No wellness language — no "self-care," "boundaries," "healing journey"
- No self-help framing — no "5 steps," no tips, no strategies
- Always points to Christ — not to a practice, not to a program, to Him
- Sender: Grace Turner <grace@sanctuary-grace.com>
- Sign-off: Come as you are. Grace

## Cadence
| Email | Day | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 | Welcome + you are not here by accident |
| 2 | Day 3 | A woman from scripture who carried what you carry |
| 3 | Day 5 | What you have been given to lay down |
| 4 | Day 7 | The brain dump — what is God saying to you? |
| 5 | Day 10 | Declarations — speak this over yourself |
| 6 | Day 14 | The next gate is ready when you are |

## Structure Per Email
- Subject line: short, sacred, no clickbait
- Opening: address her directly, name her wound without shame
- Scripture: one KJV verse with full reference, set apart as a quote
- Body: 3-5 sentences of tender, prophetic teaching from that verse
- Invitation: one soft question or invitation to sit with God
- Sign-off: Come as you are. Grace

## What to Close With (Email 6)
Preview the next gate by name. Do not pressure. "The next room is open. You will know when you are ready."
