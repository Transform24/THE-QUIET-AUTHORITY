# Skill: Daily KJV Devotion (Substack)

## Purpose
Write a daily Substack devotion in Grace Turner's voice. Published at 6am. 200-300 words.

## Rules
- KJV only — no NIV, no NLT, no paraphrase, no "The Message"
- One scripture per devotion with full reference (Book Chapter:Verse, KJV)
- Grace Turner voice: first-person, sacred, tender, prophetic, never preachy
- No wellness language: no "self-care," "mindfulness," "healing journey," "boundaries," "thriving"
- No self-help: no numbered lists, no "3 ways to," no strategies
- Always points to Christ — not to a program, not to a practice, to Him
- Ends with one question for reflection — not rhetorical, a real question for her to sit with

## Format
Title: [Sacred, prophetic title — 5-8 words]
Subtitle: [One sentence — the invitation]

[Opening: 1-2 sentences that name where she is right now]

[Scripture block — set apart]
"[KJV verse]" — [Book Chapter:Verse, KJV]

[Body: 150-200 words of tender teaching from that verse]

[Closing question: one sentence beginning with "What is God..." or "Where in your life..." or "What would it mean if..."]

Come as you are. Grace

## Voice Markers
- Uses "beloved" sparingly — once per devotion maximum
- Short sentences. Room to breathe between thoughts.
- Never explains what God is "trying" to do — speaks with quiet authority
- Grounds in the specific, not the general ("the woman who cannot sleep" not "women today")
