25 Short Teachings

Every Name, Applied

Free to read, no email required. Each name turned into one short, practical word for whatever you're carrying today.

אֶהְיֶה

Ehyeh: I AM WHO I AM

Exodus 3:14

Moses asked for a name before he'd agree to go back to Egypt. God gave him a verb, not a label. Whatever you're facing today, the answer is a present-tense God who is already in it with you, not just once, long ago.

אֵל קַנָּא

El Qanna: The Jealous God

Exodus 34:14

Not insecure jealousy. Covenant jealousy, the kind a spouse has for a spouse. If something's quietly taken the place He belongs in, this isn't an accusation, it's an invitation back to first place.

אֲדֹנָי

Adonai: Lord and Master

Used throughout Scripture

Naming who's actually in charge doesn't remove your responsibility, it removes your need to carry what was never yours to carry alone. Today's decision: hand back the one thing you've been white-knuckling.

עַתִּיק יוֹמִין

Attiq Yomin: The Ancient of Days

Daniel 7:9

Daniel saw this name in a vision about kingdoms rising and falling. Whatever feels permanent and overwhelming right now isn't older than He is. Nothing outlasts Him.

אֵל עֶלְיוֹן

El Elyon: God Most High

Genesis 14:18-20

Spoken by Melchizedek right after Abram won a battle he had no business winning. When you get a win that's bigger than your own strength explains, this is the name for who gets the credit.

אֵל גִּבּוֹר

El Gibbor: The Mighty God

Isaiah 9:6

Listed among the titles for a coming child. Might, wrapped in vulnerability. If you're waiting on strength to show up in a form you don't recognize yet, this is worth sitting with.

אֵל עוֹלָם

El Olam: The Everlasting God

Genesis 21:33

Abraham planted a tree and called on this name in a place he was only passing through. You don't need permanence in your circumstances to plant something real.

אֵל דֵּעוֹת

El Deot: The God of Knowledge

1 Samuel 2:3

Hannah's prayer, after years of being misunderstood by everyone around her. This name is for the moment someone gets your motives wrong. He doesn't.

אֵל רֳאִי

El Roi: The God Who Sees Me

Genesis 16:13

Hagar, alone, cast out, in the wilderness, named God this after believing no one was watching. If you feel invisible today, you were seen before you ever asked to be.

יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת

Yahweh Sabaoth: The Lord of Hosts

1 Samuel 1:3

Commander over armies you can't see. Whatever battle feels lopsided from where you're standing, the visible odds were never the whole picture.

יְהוָה שָׁמָּה

Yahweh Shammah: The Lord Is There

Ezekiel 48:35

The last line of a vision about a rebuilt city. Restoration isn't just about what gets fixed, it's about who doesn't leave once it is.

אֱלֹהִים

Elohim: Creator God

Genesis 1:1

The first name for God in Scripture, spoken before anything else existed. Whatever you're trying to build or rebuild, you're working with the same God who started from nothing and called it good.

אֵל מְחֹלְלֶךָ

El M'cholelecha: The God Who Fathered You

Deuteronomy 32:18

The image is of labor, of being formed on purpose, not by accident. If you've ever wondered whether you were a mistake, this verse answers that directly.

אַבָּא

Abba: Father

Romans 8:15

Not a formal title. The word a small child uses. You're not required to approach Him formally to be heard.

אֵל שַׁדַּי

El Shaddai: God Almighty

Genesis 17:1

Given to Abraham right before an impossible promise. When what you need feels biologically or logistically impossible, this is the name attached to promises that don't run on human math.

יְהוָה צִדְקֵנוּ

Yahweh Tsidkenu: The Lord Our Righteousness

Jeremiah 23:6

Not your own righteousness, His, given, not earned. If shame is running the show today, this name is the correction.

יְהוָה מְקַדִּשְׁכֶם

Yahweh M'Kaddesh: The Lord Who Sanctifies

Exodus 31:13

Being set apart isn't something you manufacture through effort. It's something done to you, by Him.

יְהוָה רֹעִי

Yahweh Rohi: The Lord Is My Shepherd

Psalm 23:1

The most quoted name for a reason: it's the one that answers "who's actually leading me" on your hardest, most ordinary days, not just your best ones.

יְהוָה יִרְאֶה

Yahweh Jireh: The Lord Will Provide

Genesis 22:14

Spoken by Abraham at the exact moment provision showed up, not before. If you're still waiting, you're not behind schedule, you're right before the name gets spoken.

יְהוָה נִסִּי

Yahweh Nissi: The Lord Is My Banner

Exodus 17:15

Raised after the battle was already won, not as a plea beforehand. Some fights are already decided. This name is for raising the flag, not begging for the outcome.

יְהוָה שָׁלוֹם

Yahweh Shalom: The Lord Is Peace

Judges 6:24

Given to Gideon, a man who was hiding in fear when God first found him. Peace here isn't the absence of a threat, it's God's presence inside one.

אֵל גְּמֻלוֹת

El Gemulot: The God of Recompense

Jeremiah 51:56

What's owed gets repaid, in His timing, not necessarily yours. If you're owed something that hasn't been made right yet, this name says it's not forgotten.

יְהוָה רֹפְאֶךָ

Yahweh Rapha: The Lord Who Heals

Exodus 15:26

Spoken right after bitter water turned sweet. Healing here follows bitterness, it doesn't skip over it.

גֹּאֵל

Go'el: The Redeemer

Job 19:25

Job said this in his worst season, not his best. You don't have to feel strong to say something true about who God is.

עִמָּנוּ אֵל

Immanu El: God With Us

Isaiah 7:14

The simplest promise in the whole set: not distant, not eventually, with you. Whatever today actually looks like, that one hasn't changed.
