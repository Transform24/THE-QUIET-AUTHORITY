Choose Your Moment

Where Are You Right Now?

Each one is short. Each one is enough.

Morning
For the moment before your feet touch the floor.

Before the phone. Before the list. Before the day gets to speak first. His mercies are new every morning, not carried over from yesterday's supply.

"My voice shalt thou hear in the morning, O LORD; in the morning will I direct my prayer unto thee, and will look up."

Psalm 5:3 · Lamentations 3:22-23 · Psalm 118:24, KJV

Morning 1:20 · A word from Grace
Midday
For the pause in the middle of the workday.

For the break when the noise has gotten loud. You are not weak for needing this moment. You are wise for taking it.

"Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God."

Philippians 4:6-7 · Matthew 11:28, KJV

Midday 1:21 · A word from Grace
Drive Home
For the drive between work and home.

Before you walk back through your own door. You do not have to arrive home still carrying today. Let this drive be the bridge, not just the distance.

"Be still, and know that I am God."

Psalm 46:10 · Psalm 23:3 · 2 Corinthians 5:17, KJV

Drive Home 1:15 · A word from Grace
Bedside
For the last moments before sleep.

When the day is finally over. Whatever was not finished today will still be there tomorrow. It does not need you tonight.

"I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety."

Psalm 4:8 · Proverbs 3:24 · Psalm 91:1, KJV

Bedside 1:26 · A word from Grace
