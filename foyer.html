The Foyer

Four Rooms. One House. Come In and Look Around.

Something in your life got loud enough, or quiet enough, or heavy enough, to bring you here. This is the entryway into Sanctuary Grace Ministry, a home for the woman who has been stopped and does not yet know that God orchestrated the stopping. Nothing is required of you in this foyer. Only your willingness to stand still long enough to hear whether there is a voice behind one of these doors.
