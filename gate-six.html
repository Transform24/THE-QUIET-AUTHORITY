Tool Two

The New Name

Four women entered the Circle of Silence.
Which one is walking out?

The Guilty Giver becomes the Grace Giver
She now pours from overflow, not depletion.

She used to give out of obligation and fear of being found lacking. The Grace Giver gives because she has first been filled. Nothing is taken from her that she did not freely hand over.

The Depleted Survivor becomes the Restored Woman
She is no longer just surviving.

Survival was a faithful chapter, but it was never the whole story. The Restored Woman is not defined by what she endured. She is defined by what God has rebuilt in the place where she endured it.

The Striving Achiever becomes the Surrendered Leader
She leads from rest, not performance.

She still leads, and she still builds. What has changed is the source. The Surrendered Leader no longer proves her worth through output. She works from a settled place that nothing she accomplishes can add to or take away.

The Lost Wanderer becomes the Found Woman
She dwells in the secret place.

She was looking for a place to belong, and she found out the place had been prepared for her. The Found Woman does not wander back out. She knows the way home, and she knows the One who is waiting there.

"Neither shall thy name any more be called Abram, but thy name shall be Abraham; for a father of many nations have I made thee."

Genesis 17:5, KJV

"And he said, Thy name shall be called no more Jacob, but Israel: for as a prince hast thou power with God and with men, and hast prevailed."

Genesis 32:28, KJV

"And when Jesus beheld him, he said, Thou art Simon the son of Jona: thou shalt be called Cephas, which is by interpretation, A stone."

John 1:42, KJV

Abram became Abraham. Jacob became Israel. Simon became Peter. A new name does not erase the old one. It declares that something fundamental has shifted.

She did not become someone else. She became who He always said she was.
