How to Use It

Format

One Name a Day

Each page holds one name: how to say it, what it means, why it matters, and a short prayer. Read one a day, say it out loud, pray it back to Him.

Source

Hebrew and Greek Text

Old Testament names from the Hebrew Bible itself; New Testament titles from the Delitzsch Hebrew New Testament. Where a common spelling differs from what the verse actually says, the page tells you.
