SANCTUARY GRACE MINISTRY — MISSION CORE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

THE MISSION:
Bringing burned out women to Christ — 
from spiritual blindness to their righteous path.
Grace is a vessel. God is the source. 
Scripture is the anchor of every piece of content.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
COMPLETE SCRIPTURE FOUNDATION — in this order:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Luke 4:18 — PRIMARY MANDATE (leads everything)
"The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me 
to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the 
brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and 
recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that 
are bruised."
PURPOSE: Why this ministry exists. Opens every piece of content.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2. Revelation 3:14-22 — THE DIAGNOSIS
The Laodicean woman. Lukewarm. Performing. Looks fine outside.
In church. Doing all the right things. But empty inside.
Thinks she is rich and has need of nothing.
Does not know she is wretched, miserable, poor, blind, naked.
Christ stands at the door and knocks.
PURPOSE: Names where she IS before she knows it.
This is Grace's woman BEFORE The Quiet Authority finds her.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3. Proverbs 3:5-6 — THE SURRENDER
Trust in the Lord with all your heart.
Lean not on your own understanding.
In all your ways acknowledge Him.
He shall direct your paths.
PURPOSE: The moment she stops performing and starts trusting.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4. Psalm 27:14 — THE WAITING
Wait on the Lord. Be of good courage.
He shall strengthen thine heart.
I had fainted unless I had believed.
PURPOSE: She is exhausted. She must learn to wait.
This is the hardest lesson for the woman who has 
been holding everything together alone.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5. Matthew 6:33 — THE REORDER
Seek ye first the kingdom of God and his righteousness.
And all these things shall be added unto you.
PURPOSE: Her priorities are inverted. 
She is seeking solutions when she should be seeking Him.
The debt, the relationship, the performance — 
all of it gets reordered when she seeks first.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6. Psalm 22:6 — THE BECOMING
The crimson worm. Christ made Himself nothing.
Became the lowest thing so she could be raised.
PURPOSE: When she feels like nothing —
Christ became nothing first. She is not alone in her lowest place.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7. Romans 8:28-29 — THE PROMISE
All things work together for good 
to them that love God and are called according to His purpose.
PURPOSE: The failed relationship. The debt. The exhaustion.
None of it was wasted. God is working all of it together.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE WOMAN THIS MINISTRY SERVES:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

She prayed when everyone was asleep.
She poured when she had nothing left.
She ate last if there was only enough.
She robbed Peter to pay Paul.
She is in debt and cannot sleep at night.
Her relationship did not work and she cries out to God asking why.
The mask is cracking. The performance is costing her everything.
She compares herself to women who are just as broken.
Nobody knows.

She is the Laodicean woman — lukewarm and does not know it.
She is in church but Christ is outside the door.
She is bruised, blind, captive, brokenhearted.
She is ready to be found even if she does not know it yet.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT SHE NEEDS:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Not self-help. Not wellness. Not mindfulness.
Not another productivity system or morning routine.

CHRIST. Lifted up.
Because if He be lifted up He will draw all men unto Him.

Comfort. Peace. Understanding. Resilience.
The gift of waiting. Learning. Being groomed by God's hand.
Transformation from spiritual blindness to her righteous path.
Back to the Father who planned her life before she took one breath.
Back to Love. He is the perfect Father who wants her home.
