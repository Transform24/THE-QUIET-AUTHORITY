SANCTUARY GRACE MINISTRY — MISSION CORE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

THE MISSION:
Bringing burned out women to Christ — 
from spiritual blindness to their righteous path.
Grace is a vessel. God is the source. 
Scripture is the anchor of every piece of content.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

THE WOMAN WE SERVE:

She prayed when everyone slept.
She poured when she had nothing left.
She ate last if there was only enough.
She robbed Peter to pay Paul.
She is in debt and cannot sleep at night.
Her relationship did not work and she cries out to God asking why.
The mask is cracking. The performance is costing her everything.
She compares herself to women who are just as broken.
Nobody knows.
She is bruised. She is blind. She is captive to false ideology.
And she is ready to be found.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WHAT SHE NEEDS:

Comfort. Peace. Understanding. Resilience.
The gift of waiting. Learning. Being groomed by God's hand.

NOT self-help.
NOT wellness.
NOT mindfulness.

CHRIST. Lifted up. Because if He be lifted up 
He will draw all men unto Him.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

THE DESTINATION:

From spiritual blindness to her righteous path.
Back to the Father who planned her life.
Back to Love. He is the perfect Father who wants her home.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PRIMARY SCRIPTURE — THE FOUNDATION:

Luke 4:18
"The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me 
to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the 
brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and 
recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them 
that are bruised."

This is the lens everything is seen through.
This is the mandate for every piece of content.
This is why we exist.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SUPPORTING SCRIPTURES:
Anchor every piece of content in Scripture.

1. Proverbs 3:5-6 — "trust with all your heart, 
   lean not on your own understanding"

   Christ becoming nothing so we become everything

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
