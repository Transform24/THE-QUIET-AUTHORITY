Tool Four

How to Set Up Your Secret Place

The physical space matters. What surrounds you shapes what you hear.
You do not need much. You need intention.

Step 01
Choose your location

A chair. A corner. A closet. A car. The room does not have to be special — your intention makes it sacred. Return to the same place. Your soul will begin to recognize it.

Step 02
Remove the noise

Phone on silent. Notifications off. This is not the time to be available to anyone but God. Even 15 minutes fully given is worth more than an hour half-present.

Step 03
Anchor your senses

A candle. A journal. A worn Bible. A cup of something warm. Physical anchors tell your body it is safe to slow down. Over time these become the cues that open you.

Step 04
Enter in this order

Worship first — even one song, even humming. Then the Word. Then prayer. Then wait. Do not rush the waiting. The waiting is where He speaks.

"Worship. Word. Prayer. Wait. The silence after the prayer is not empty. That is where He is."
