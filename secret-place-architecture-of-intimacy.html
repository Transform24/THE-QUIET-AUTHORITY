The Lineage of Quiet Defiance

None of these women lived in an emergency tent

Shiphrah and Puah feared God, and that reverence made Pharaoh's threats powerless. Jehosheba hid a prince in the temple for six years. Jael ended a battle inside her own tent. Rizpah sat on a rock for months and forced a national response. The woman with the alabaster box poured out an expensive gift in front of critics who judged her by cost. Mary Magdalene stayed at the cross when the others scattered, and came to the tomb first, while it was still dark.

They held their ground from a position of deep interior stillness. Stepping into the secret place is not an escape from your life. It is how you take your place in that same line of women.
