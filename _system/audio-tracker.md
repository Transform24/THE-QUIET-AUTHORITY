## 2. Starter credits treasury law

Grace's rule (2026-10-13). A voice asset may be generated only after its script text has passed the final human validation gate. In order:
1. The script text is written or drafted. Drafting by an agent never generates audio.
2. Grace reads the final text and says yes in her own words.
3. The approval is written next to the text (the date and her yes).
4. Only then may one generation be started, and only for that approved text.
5. No automatic job, schedule or workflow may call a voice service. No test generations on unapproved text.
6. Any cost still needs Grace's approval first (mandate 5 of the blueprint).

The monthly starter limit is spent only on approved text. If a draft is not approved, no credits are used.
