**Reflections that type out on screen**
Psalm 91:1-2: Seter is not a visit. It is a dwelling. The Hebrew word behind this verse is seter, a hiding place, a shelter you stay inside rather than duck into. God was never meant to be an emergency tent. The secret place was built to be lived in.
Psalm 91:4: Feathers are close, and warm, and soft. He does not keep you safe from a distance. He covers you the way a mother bird covers her young, and His truth stands in front of you like a shield. You can stop holding up your own.
Psalm 91:14-15: Notice who is speaking. It is God, and He says He will answer. He does not say you will never be in trouble. He says He will be with you in it. Call upon Him, even when you have only a few words left.
