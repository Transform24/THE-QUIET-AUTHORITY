# The Sound of His Knock

"Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if any man hear my voice, and open the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me." Revelation 3:20

You have been performing so long you forgot what it feels like to simply be with Him. All those right answers in Bible study. All that service in the name of Jesus. All those smiles when you were breaking inside. And still—the exhaustion. Still—the emptiness that wakes you at 3 a.m. wondering how you became a woman who has everything to give everyone else but nothing left for herself.

He is not asking you to do more. He is knocking. Not demanding entry. Not listing your failures. Not reminding you of all the ways you have been lukewarm while looking faithful. Just knocking. Waiting. The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to heal the brokenhearted, to set at liberty them that are bruised.

What if the door you need to open is not to another ministry, another responsibility, another way to prove your worth? What if the door is to your own broken heart—the place you have kept locked because you believed you had to hold it all together? He wants to come in. Not to inspect. To sup with you. To sit at your table in the mess of your life and simply be with you.

You do not have to clean up first. You do not have to fix yourself before He will enter.
