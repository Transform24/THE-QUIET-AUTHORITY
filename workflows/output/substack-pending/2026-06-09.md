# When the Doing Becomes Your Undoing

"The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised." — Luke 4:18

I see you there, friend. You've been so good at the doing. The serving, the showing up, the making it work when it shouldn't work anymore. You've built something that looks like faithfulness from the outside, but inside you know the truth. You are so tired you can taste it. The doing has become your undoing.

Christ did not come to give you a better system for managing your exhaustion. He came to heal the brokenhearted. To set at liberty them that are bruised. That is you. Not the version of you that has it all together, but the one who cannot sleep at night because the weight of it all crushes your chest. The one who wonders if God sees how hard you've tried, how much you've sacrificed, how little is left.

He does see. And He is not asking you to do more. He is asking you to stop long enough to let Him bind up what is broken. To let Him carry what you were never meant to carry alone. The doing was never meant to save you. Only He can do that.

What if today you set down just one thing? Not to fix yourself, but to make room for Him to do what only He can do.
