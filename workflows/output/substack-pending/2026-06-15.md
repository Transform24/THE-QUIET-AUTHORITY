# The Debt You Cannot Pay

"The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised." — Luke 4:18

You know the weight of owing what you cannot repay. The numbers that wake you at three in the morning. The statements you cannot bear to open. The shame that follows you through every transaction, every declined card, every careful calculation at the grocery store.

But there is another debt, older and deeper than any balance you carry. It is the debt of trying to earn your worth through perfect performance. Of believing you must pay your way into God's presence through service, sacrifice, and self-erasure. You have been trying to settle an account that was marked "paid in full" two thousand years ago on a cross.

Christ did not come to give you a payment plan. He came to cancel the debt entirely. Not because you finally got it together. Not because you served enough, gave enough, or became enough. But because He chose to pay what you could never pay, to become poor so that you might become rich in Him.

The captivity you feel is real. But the deliverance He offers is not contingent on your credit score, your productivity, or your ability to hold it all together.

Will you let Him heal what is truly broken? Will you bring Him the debt you have been hiding?
