# The Debt You Cannot Carry

"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest." Matthew 11:28

You have been adding it up in the dark. The numbers that do not reconcile. The bills that arrived before you could breathe from the last ones. The interest that compounds while you lie awake, calculating paths that all end in the same impossible place. You have carried this alone because you believed you had to. Because somewhere along the way, you learned that your worth was measured by your ability to hold it all together.

But that crushing weight on your chest at 3 AM, that tightness in your throat when you open the mailbox, that shame that tells you to hide—none of it was meant for you to bear. Christ did not die so you could carry your own debt. He died because the debt itself is too heavy for any human shoulders. Not just the financial weight, but the burden of believing you must save yourself.

He is not asking you to fix it first. He is not waiting for you to present Him with a budget that works or a plan that makes sense. He sees you in the sleepless arithmetic of your fear, and He is saying: Come. Bring the impossible numbers. Bring the shame. Bring the crushing weight of what you owe and cannot pay.

What if surrender is not failure, but the only honest math that ever mattered?
