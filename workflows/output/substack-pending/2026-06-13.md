# The Debt That Was Paid

"The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised." — Luke 4:18

You carry debts that wake you at three in the morning. The ones with numbers attached, yes. But also the unpayable ones. The debt of being enough for everyone who needs you. The debt of keeping it all together. The debt of having failed at both.

I know the ledger you keep in your mind. How you've borrowed from yourself until there is nothing left. How you've tried to balance the books by working harder, giving more, being better. How the interest compounds in sleepless nights and a heart that cannot remember what rest feels like.

Christ did not come to help you manage your debt. He came to pay it. "It is finished," He said. Not "try harder now." Not "here are seven steps." Finished. The ledger is closed. The account is settled. Every debt you owe for not being enough, for failing, for falling short—He took it to the cross and declared it paid in full.

This is not metaphor. This is the gospel. You are not a project to be improved. You are a daughter to be redeemed.

What if today, you stopped trying to earn what has already been given? What if you let Him carry what you were never meant to hold?
