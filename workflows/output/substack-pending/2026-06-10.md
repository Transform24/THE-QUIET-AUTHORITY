# The Debt That Taught Me to Pray

"The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised." Luke 4:18

I remember the night I couldn't sleep because the numbers wouldn't stop adding themselves in my head. Every credit card balance. Every missed opportunity to say no. Every sacrifice I made that was supposed to prove I was good enough, generous enough, faithful enough. The debt wasn't just financial. I had been spending myself into poverty for years, pouring out what I didn't have, performing a version of abundance while my soul grew thin.

That's when I learned what the poor in spirit really means. Not just broke. Broken open. Empty of my own solutions. Stripped of the carefully managed image. Christ came to preach the gospel to the poor because only the poor know they need rescue. Only the bankrupt stop pretending they can save themselves.

He didn't come with a budget plan or a wellness strategy. He came to heal what was brokenhearted. To set free what was captive to shame and striving. To give sight to those of us who have been blind to our own belovedness.

If you are awake tonight counting what you owe, listen: He sees you. Not to condemn the debt, but to deliver you from the slavery of earning what has already been given.
