# The Debt You Cannot Pay

"The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised." — Luke 4:18

There is a debt you have been trying to pay that was never yours to carry. The ledger in your mind that keeps you awake at 3 a.m.—the one where you owe everyone everything and can never give enough. You have been pouring yourself out until the vessel cracked, and still you feel like a debtor.

I know you have calculated it all. The money you do not have. The energy you cannot muster. The version of yourself you promised to be but cannot deliver. You have been both the servant and the slave, working to earn what you believe you must deserve. But Christ did not come to help you budget your way to worthiness. He came to cancel the debt entirely.

"And you, being dead in your sins and the uncircumcision of your flesh, hath he quickened together with him, having forgiven you all trespasses; Blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us, which was contrary to us, and took it out of the way, nailing it to his cross." — Colossians 2:13-14

He took the ledger to the cross. Every sleepless calculation. Every failure you replay. Every dollar you do not have. He did not come to make you a better manager of your brokenness. He came to set the captive free.

You do not owe Him your performance. You owe Him your surrender.
