# The Door You Cannot Open

"Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if any man hear my voice, and open the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me." Revelation 3:20

You have opened every other door with your own hands. The door to provision when there was no money. The door to peace when everyone else was falling apart. The door to strength when your body had nothing left. You have been your own savior so long, you forgot what it means to be saved.

But this door will not yield to your effort. Christ stands on the other side, knocking. Not demanding. Not forcing. Knocking. He will not break it down, though He has every right. He waits for you to stop holding it closed with the weight of your competence, your grinding, your terror that if you let go, everything will collapse.

What if the collapse is the mercy? What if He has been waiting for you to run out of strength so you would finally open the door not as the woman who manages everything, but as the one who needs Him?

He does not come to admire your table. He comes to sup with you. To sit. To break bread. To be with you in the quiet you have been too afraid to enter.

You do not have to clean the house first. You do not have to have it all together. Just open the door.
