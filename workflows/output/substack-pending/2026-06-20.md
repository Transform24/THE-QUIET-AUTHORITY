# The Door You've Been Guarding

"Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if any man hear my voice, and open the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me." Revelation 3:20

You've been standing guard at that door for so long. The one between your carefully managed exterior and the chaos you hide inside. The sleepless nights. The account you check at 3am. The prayers you whisper in the shower because it's the only place no one needs you.

You learned early that keeping that door closed was survival. That if anyone saw the real numbers, the real exhaustion, the real doubt, they'd know you've been running on empty for years. So you've kept performing. Kept serving. Kept saying yes when your body was screaming no. You thought that's what faithfulness looked like.

But He's not standing at your door with a clipboard, ready to audit your failures. He's not waiting for you to clean up first, to get your finances in order, to figure out how you let it get this bad. He's knocking because He knows exactly what's on the other side of that door, and He's asking you to let Him in anyway. Not to fix you like a project. To sup with you. To sit in the mess and be present.

What if the door you've been guarding so carefully is the very place He wants to meet you?
