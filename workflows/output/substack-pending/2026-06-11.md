# The Door You've Been Guarding

"Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if any man hear my voice, and open the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me." Revelation 3:20

You've been standing on the other side of that door for so long now. You can hear Him knocking, but you're afraid of what He'll see when you open it. The mess you've made. The debt you're drowning in. The person you've become while trying to hold everything together. You think if you can just clean up a little more, get a little stronger, fix a few more things, then you'll be ready to let Him in.

But He's not knocking because He doesn't know what's on the other side. He knows about the sleepless nights, the calculations you run at three in the morning, the way you've poured yourself out until there's nothing left. He knows you've been performing righteousness while starving inside. And still He knocks. Not with judgment. With an invitation to sit with you in the middle of it all.

The door He's knocking on isn't the entrance to your perfect life. It's the door to your actual one. The one where you're barely holding on. Where you've sacrificed yourself empty trying to be enough. He wants in exactly as it is, because He came to heal the brokenhearted and set at liberty them that are bruised.

What if you stopped guarding the door and simply opened it?
