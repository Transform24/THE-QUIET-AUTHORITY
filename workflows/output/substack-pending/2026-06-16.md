# The Door You've Been Guarding

"Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if any man hear my voice, and open the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me." Revelation 3:20

You have been so careful with that door. You open it just enough to let people see what they need to see—the capable woman, the one who manages, the one who never quite breaks. You have learned to answer the knock with a smile that costs you everything, to serve from rooms in your heart that have been empty for years.

But listen. The knock you hear in the sleepless hours, the one that comes when you finally stop moving, when the house is quiet and the mask comes off—that is not another demand. That is not someone else who needs you to be strong. It is Christ, and He is not asking you to open the door so He can add to your burden. He is asking to come in and sit with you in the rooms you have been hiding. The ones where the bills are stacked and the prayers feel desperate and you don't know how much longer you can do this.

He does not need you to clean up first. He does not need you to have it together. He has been standing there, knocking gently, while you exhausted yourself trying to be enough. What if today you just opened the door?

Will you let Him in—not as another task, but as the rest you have been aching for?
