The Silence That Speaks

Romans 3:25 — God presented Him as a sacrifice of atonement, through faith in His blood.

There is a depth in quiet that noise cannot touch. In the stillness of prayer, in the waiting before words come, in the space between breath and heartbeat — this is where God meets us not with answers, but with presence.

You have been carrying so much. The weight of trying. The ache of not being enough. The weight of wanting to do it all right, be it all for everyone. And in the midst of that carrying, there is a whisper: Come. Rest here. Not when you've earned it. Not after you've finished. Now.

The sacrifice has been made. Not because of what you do or achieve, but because of who you are — beloved, seen, held in the Father's eternal gaze.

What would it mean to believe, just for today, that you are enough?
