## What changed, as you asked

1. The scripture now appears one word at a time, slower (80 words a minute).
2. The reflection types out slowly after a 4 second quiet.
3. The change between verses is short. Each scene rests 8 seconds and dissolves in 1 second.
4. Romans 10:9-10 (KJV) closes the teaching and stays on the screen while the prayer settles in the lower third for 1 minute 53 seconds.
5. The "Thank you for watching." page follows, with the same words as Version 8. The music and picture fade out together.

## The order is now fixed for every video

Intro, teaching blocks, Romans 10:9-10 with the prayer, thank you page. A new video changes only the scriptures, the reflections and the prayer. The pace rules are in one file and the checker stops the render if a rule or a verse is off.
