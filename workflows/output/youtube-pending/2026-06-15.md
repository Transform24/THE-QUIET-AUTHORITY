# SCRIPT TITLE
When Sunday Feels Like Performance

---

## FULL SCRIPT

**[OPENING — 30 seconds]**

It's Sunday morning. You're in the parking lot, checking your reflection one more time. Practicing your smile. Rehearsing the answer to "how are you doing?" — the one that sounds faithful enough to pass.

Inside, you're wondering if anyone can see through it. The sleepless nights. The checking account you're afraid to open. The prayers you can barely whisper anymore because you're too tired to hope they'll be heard.

The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised. — Luke 4:18

This is for you.

**[TEACHING — 2 minutes]**

There's a letter in Revelation written to a church that had everything together on the outside. They were busy. Faithful in appearance. Doing all the right things.

And Jesus said this to them:

*"I know thy works, that thou art neither cold nor hot: I would thou wert cold or hot. So then because thou art lukewarm, and neither cold nor hot, I will spue thee out of my mouth. Because thou sayest, I am rich, and increased with goods, and have need of nothing; and knowest not that thou art wretched, and miserable, and poor, and blind, and naked."* — Revelation 3:15-17

He wasn't condemning them. He was diagnosing them.

They had become performers. Going through the motions. Looking the part while dying inside.

And then He says something that changes everything:

*"Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if any man hear my voice, and open the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me."* — Revelation 3:20

He's not waiting for you to fix yourself first. He's not asking you to get it together before you answer. He's knocking. Right now. While you're still tired. Still in debt. Still unsure how you'll make it through the week.

He's not asking for a performance. He's asking for the door to open.

What if Sunday wasn't about showing up with the right answer? What if it was about coming honest — broken, empty, barely holding on — and letting Him meet you there?

You don't have to pretend anymore. Not with Him. He already knows what the sleepless nights are about. He sees the burden you're carrying in secret. And He's still knocking.

Not because you've earned it. But because He is your Redeemer.

**[CLOSE — 30 seconds]**

You don't need another strategy to hold yourself together. You need the One who holds you when you can't.

Stop performing. Open the door.

Come as you are.

## SEO DESCRIPTION

Sunday morning shouldn't feel like a performance, but for so many women carrying hidden burdens — sleepless nights, financial fear, exhaustion from pouring out when there's nothing left — church has become one more place to pretend everything is fine.

In Revelation 3, Jesus speaks to the Laodicean church — a people who looked faithful on the outside but were lukewarm within. They had everything together in appearance, yet Christ called them wretched, poor, blind, and naked. But He didn't leave them there. He stood at the door and knocked, offering not judgment, but communion.

This video is for the woman who checks her reflection in the parking lot before walking into church. The one rehearsing her answer to "how are you?" so it sounds faithful enough. The one too tired to hope her prayers are even heard anymore.

You don't have to perform for Him. He already knows. And He's still knocking — not waiting for you to fix yourself first, but inviting you to open the door exactly as you are: tired, broken, barely holding on.

Come honest. Come empty. Come as you are.
