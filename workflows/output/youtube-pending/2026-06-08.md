# SCRIPT TITLE
When Everything You Built Is Burning Down

---

## FULL SCRIPT

**[OPENING — 30 seconds]**

"The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised." Luke 4:18.

You built something with your own hands. A career. A ministry. A family system. A reputation. You mortared every brick with your own strength, your own wisdom, your own relentless effort.

And now it's burning.

---

**[TEACHING — 2 minutes]**

The bank account is overdrawn. The relationship is fractured. The body finally said no. The thing you were certain God blessed is now collapsing, and you lie awake at three in the morning wondering if you missed Him entirely.

Let me tell you what's actually happening.

God is not punishing you. He is delivering you.

Proverbs 3:5-6 says: "Trust in the Lord with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths."

You have been leaning on your own understanding for so long that you forgot what it felt like to be held. You have been directing your own paths with such determination that you never noticed He was trying to redirect you.

And so He allowed the fire.

Not because He is cruel. Because He is kind. Because you would never have let go if it hadn't burned.

The Laodicean woman in Revelation 3 thought she had need of nothing. She was rich, she was productive, she was performing flawlessly. And Christ said, "You are wretched, and miserable, and poor, and blind, and naked."

She could not see her own poverty until everything she built with her own hands was exposed as wood, hay, and stubble.

You are not losing everything. You are losing everything that was never meant to hold you.

The marriage you controlled. The ministry you built in your own name. The business you funded with your own glory. The identity you stitched together from other people's approval.

It had to burn. Because underneath all of it, Christ has been standing at the door and knocking.

He doesn't want your productivity. He wants you.

He doesn't want you to rebuild faster. He wants you to stop building altogether and let Him be the foundation you never had.

Romans 8:28 promises: "And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose."

All things. Even the fire. Even the failure. Even the financial ruin and the sleepless nights and the humiliation of starting over.

He is working it together. Not to restore what you lost. But to reveal what you never had—Him as your portion, your provider, your peace.

---

**[CLOSE — 30 seconds]**

So let it burn, beloved.

Stop trying to salvage what God is trying to sanctify.

Stop trying to rebuild what He is trying to redeem.

Come to Him empty. Come to Him broken. Come to Him with nothing left but your need.

That is where He meets you. That is where He always meets you.

Come as you are.
