## SCRIPT TITLE
When Exhaustion Becomes an Invitation

## FULL VIDEO SCRIPT

**OPENING (30 seconds)**
Hi, beloved. Before we begin, I'd like to invite you to take a deep breath. To arrive here, just as you are. Not with your best self, not the version you wish you were. But you—tired, questioning, maybe doubting. That's who this is for.

**MAIN TEACHING (8-10 minutes)**
I want to talk to you about a word I think gets misunderstood in our faith tradition. Exhaustion. We treat it like a failure. Like we didn't trust enough, didn't pray enough, didn't have enough faith. And so we hide it. We push through it. We smile at church and cry in our cars.

But what if exhaustion isn't a sign that we're failing? What if it's an invitation?

Look at these words from Matthew 11:28. Jesus says: "Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest."

He doesn't say, "Come to me when you've figured it out. Come to me when you're strong enough. Come to me when you've prayed enough." He says come as you are. Weary. Burdened. Exhausted.

The women I talk to most often are doing everything right. You love God. You show up for your family. You serve your church. You try to be enough for everyone. And somewhere in that trying, in that showing up, in that serving—you lost yourself.

That exhaustion? That's not failure. That's wisdom. It's your soul saying, I need something different. I need rest that isn't lazy. I need stillness that isn't selfish. I need someone to tell me that I'm enough, not because of what I do, but because of who I am.

This is what The Quiet Authority is about. Not hustle. Not self-improvement. But coming home to yourself. To the woman God made you to be. Before anyone asked anything of you.

**SILENCE INVITATION (2 minutes)**
So I want to invite you into something right now. You don't have to do anything. You don't have to say anything. You don't have to be strong or put together. Just be here with me. Close your eyes if you'd like.

Breathe in slowly. God is present. Breathe out slowly. You are safe. Breathe in. You are seen. Breathe out. You are enough.

There's no performance here. No striving. Just two women—you and me—sitting in the presence of a God who loves us not for our accomplishment, but for our existence.

**CLOSING (30 seconds)**
You are beloved. Not because of what you do. Because of who you are. And if you want to explore that more deeply, if you want to discover which archetype of exhaustion you carry—and more importantly, which path is designed for exactly where you are—I'd love to invite you to the assessment. It's free. It's sacred. It's designed for you.

Come as you are. https://sanctuary-grace.com/
