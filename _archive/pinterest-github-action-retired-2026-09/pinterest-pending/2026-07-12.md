She did not know how to receive. Every gift felt like a debt. Every act of kindness toward her required immediate repayment. She had been taught, somewhere along the way, that needing was weakness and that the godly woman was always the one giving.

So she gave. And gave. And gave.

Until the day she could not remember who she was when she was not being useful to someone else.
