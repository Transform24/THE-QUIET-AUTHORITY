The One Word

Seter is not a visit. It is a dwelling.

The Hebrew word behind Psalm 91:1 is seter, a hiding place, a covering, a shelter you stay inside rather than duck into. Most of us treat God like an emergency tent, something we crawl toward when the storm hits and leave the moment it passes. Seter was never built to be visited. It was built to be lived in.

That distinction is the whole hinge of the study waiting behind this page. Not another five-minute devotional to check off, but five days of learning to stay.

A Free Reflection

Leaving the Emergency Tent

A short teaching, free, from Day 1 of the full study.

Think of the last time you prayed hardest. Was it in a crisis? For most of us, prayer gets loudest exactly when life gets loudest, then quiets back down the moment things settle. That is the emergency tent. It works. It is also not what Psalm 91:1 is describing. "He that dwelleth" is present tense, ongoing, a woman who has made the secret place her address, not her ambulance.
